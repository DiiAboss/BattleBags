# Unnamed Match Game - Game Design Document

## Overview
A fast-paced, strategic match-based puzzle game emphasizing combos, resource management, and dynamic gameplay. Set in a desolate post-apocalyptic landscape, players gather garbage and recycle it into valuable resources using drones and factories.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Match-3 Core**: Match blocks horizontally or vertically to clear them.
- **Combos**: Achieving multiple consecutive matches grants bonuses and special effects.
- **Drone Mechanics**: Diverse drones handle collection, transportation, sorting, and offensive tasks.
- **Resource Management**: Convert matched blocks into resources and upgrades.

## Progression System
- **Drone Upgrades**: Enhance drone attributes (speed, capacity, abilities).
- **Factory Upgrades**: Improve efficiency, resource output, and unlock advanced features.
- **Skill Tree**: Unlock skills and abilities, enhancing strategic depth.

## Timed Objectives
- Objectives provide upgrade bonuses and currency:
  - Destroy 25 green blocks within 1 minute.
  - Achieve a 10x combo within 25 moves.
  - Complete various dynamically generated challenges.

## Shops
- Appear randomly on the map and always before boss encounters.
- Offer 3-5 major factory/drone upgrades, 1-3 drones.
- Always includes an expensive, highly rewarding item.

## Boss Battles
- Engage AI-controlled opponents on dual-grid interfaces.
- Use drones to drop garbage blocks onto the opponent's grid.
- Defeated bosses can be assimilated as factory workers or released to grow stronger and yield better upgrades upon future encounters.

## Glitches and Malfunctions
- Random events that temporarily alter gameplay, such as:
  - Freezing blocks on landing.
  - Slime-covered blocks.
  - Reduced visibility due to EMP.
- Act as temporary enemies, mitigable via drones or player actions.

## Game Modes

### Single Player
- **Main Game**: Campaign-driven, progression-based experience.

### Multiplayer (Local)
- **Factory Battles**: Competitive mode (1v1, 2v2, 4-player FFA).
- **Classic Battle**: Competitive mode without upgrades.

### Multiplayer (Online)
- **Factory Battles**: Competitive matches with upgrades.
- **Classic Battles**: Competitive matches without upgrades.
- **30 Player Battle Royale**: Massive competitive matches (decision pending on upgrades inclusion).

### AI Modes
- All multiplayer modes feature AI opponents adjustable by difficulty for solo practice.

## Visual and Audio Style
- **Visuals**: Jet Set Radio meets Persona in minimal pixel art style, vibrant and dystopian.
- **Audio**: Dynamic, responsive soundtrack, industrial and energetic.

## Player Goals and Experience
- Reward player skill, strategic thinking, and adaptability.
- Provide dynamic replayability through randomized upgrades, glitches, and drone variety.
- Incentivize timely completion of objectives for maximum reward.

## Drones and Factory Mechanics
- **Drone Types**:
  - Basic (standard functions)
  - Attack (offensive)
  - Gatherer (collect resources)
  - Conveyor (transport blocks)
  - Sorting (organize resources)
- **Drone Merging**: Combine drones of the same type to create advanced variants.
- **Factory Parts and Upgrades**:
  - Recycler: Spawns blocks with upgradeable mechanics (angles, speed, special blocks).
  - Factory Upgrades include board speed modifiers, luck adjustments, currency generation, drone performance improvements.

## Block Mechanics
- Blocks can gain special mods/upgrades:
  - Bombs (explode upon matching)
  - Arrows (fly in specified directions upon matching)
- Falling blocks destroy or interact uniquely based on their speed and match condition.
- "Big blocks" with unique modifiers affecting points, effects, and gameplay dynamics.

## Special Cursed Mods
- Alter fundamental gameplay temporarily for risk-reward mechanics, such as:
  - Blocks only move when player makes an action.
  - Blocks require manual activation to pop.
  - Increased combo points based on black blocks.

This document establishes the game's foundational vision, ensuring focused development and consistent player engagement through varied and strategic gameplay experiences.
